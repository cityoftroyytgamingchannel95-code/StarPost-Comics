What is Starpost Comics?

Starpost Comics is a indie comic publishing site for comics/mangas created by StarPost! (me!, aka Tory)

Why?

Since big comic publishing platforms have certain rules, it won't allow me to be all free. But with my own website, I can publish whatever I want by my own rules! Another reason is so I can show EXCLUSIVE stuff like art works, specials, and more!
